Output exactly SIX isolated individual photographs on a regular 3-column by 2-row grid in a 1536×1024 landscape canvas, true TRANSPARENT alpha, large empty gaps so no figures touch. Fill these genuinely different incremental body poses in reading order: TOP LEFT (A) sitting at laptop, starts leaning forward on his own left side with hands beginning to lift; TOP MIDDLE (B) leans further over the table with both palms forward, shoulders moving briefly down, elbows bent; TOP RIGHT (C) presses away from the desk, pivots slightly forward from the hips, shoulders beginning to rise, elbows still bent, head looking ahead. Subtle believable human motion and relaxed face. The hands reach toward an invisible desk, never draw the desk. Keep all faces the same; no props, no background gradients, no cast shadows outside silhouette, no captions, no grids or borders. Professional cutout asset.
